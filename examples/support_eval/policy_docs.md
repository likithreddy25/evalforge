# Nestly Support Policy

At Nestly, we strive to provide a seamless experience for both guests and hosts. This Support Policy outlines our procedures and guidelines for addressing common issues. Please read carefully to understand your rights and responsibilities.

## Refunds
1. Guests may request a full refund if a cancellation is made within 48 hours of booking, provided the check-in date is at least 14 days away.
2. For cancellations made between 7 to 14 days before check-in, guests are eligible for a 50% refund of the total booking fee.
3. No refunds will be issued for cancellations made less than 7 days before check-in unless under extenuating circumstances, which must be documented and submitted within 48 hours of cancellation.
4. Refund requests must be submitted through the Nestly platform within the specified time frames to be considered.
5. For issues not resolved within 3 business days, escalate the matter to a human support agent.

## Cancellations
1. Guests can cancel their booking via the Nestly app or website before check-in; the cancellation policy selected at the time of booking will apply.
2. Hosts must notify guests of any cancellations at least 48 hours before the scheduled check-in to avoid penalties.
3. Hosts who cancel bookings within 48 hours of check-in will incur a penalty fee of 25% of the total booking amount.
4. Guests may receive a rebooking credit equivalent to the amount paid for canceled bookings by hosts.
5. If a cancellation issue is unresolved after 5 business days, escalate to a human support agent.

## Host No-Show
1. If a host fails to show up for the scheduled check-in, guests should wait for a maximum of 2 hours before seeking assistance.
2. Guests must attempt to contact the host through the Nestly messaging system during the waiting period.
3. After 2 hours, if the host is unresponsive, guests should report the issue to Nestly customer support.
4. Guests will be eligible for a full refund if the host is unresponsive and a new booking is made elsewhere within 24 hours.
5. If the host no-show issue is not resolved within 3 business days, escalate to a human support agent.

## Safety Concern
1. Guests should immediately report any safety concerns through the Nestly app, providing details and evidence where possible.
2. For urgent safety issues, guests must contact local authorities before notifying Nestly.
3. Nestly will respond to safety concerns within 24 hours after the initial report.
4. If there is an ongoing safety issue, escalate to a human support agent without delay.
5. Guests can provide feedback on safety concerns to improve community standards.

## Account Access
1. Users must reset their password using the "Forgot Password?" link if they cannot access their account.
2. For account lockouts due to multiple failed login attempts, users can regain access after 30 minutes or by following the verification process.
3. Users must verify their email address within 48 hours of account creation; failure to do so may result in account suspension.
4. For issues related to account access that cannot be resolved through the automated system, escalate to a human support agent.
5. Users are encouraged to enable two-factor authentication for added security.

## Payment Dispute
1. Guests or hosts may initiate a payment dispute within 14 days of the transaction date.
2. Documentation supporting the dispute must be submitted through the Nestly platform for review.
3. Nestly will investigate and respond to payment disputes within 5 business days.
4. If a resolution is not reached within 7 business days, escalate to a human support agent for further assistance.
5. All financial transactions are subject to a 3% processing fee for refunds.

## What support cannot help with
1. Legal advice or representation related to disputes between guests and hosts.
2. Tax-related questions or assistance with taxation issues.
3. Personal grievances or complaints unrelated to Nestly's services.
4. Technical issues with devices or software not related to the Nestly platform. 

Thank you for choosing Nestly! We are here to support you in making your home-sharing experience enjoyable and safe.
